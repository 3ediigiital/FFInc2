About ColdFlow Logistics

ColdFlow Logistics operates one of Northern Europe's largest independent cold storage and distribution networks, headquartered in the Port of Rotterdam. Founded in 2001, we provide bonded cold storage, refrigerated transport and full 3PL distribution services for frozen food suppliers and buyers across the continent.

Our 50,000 m³ facility operates at temperatures down to -25°C, with direct port access enabling fast container turnaround for import and export shipments.

Why Choose ColdFlow
Port-side location — direct access to Rotterdam port, Europe's largest, cuts transit time significantly
Full temperature range — chilled through deep-frozen (-25°C) in one facility
Owned reefer fleet — no third-party subcontracting for core routes
48-hour turnaround — among the fastest port-to-warehouse times in the region
Dedicated account management — single point of contact for all shipments
